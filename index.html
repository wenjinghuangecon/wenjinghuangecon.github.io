Research Interests

  • International Trade
  • International Finance
  • Chinese Foreign Aid and Overseas Lending
  • RMB Internationalization

I am a Ph.D. Candidate in Economics at The Chinese University of Hong Kong, expecting to graduate in 2027. My research uses loan-level data on Chinese overseas lending and foreign aid to study how official credit shapes trade and currency choice in developing countries. My current work examines whether Chinese overseas lending promotes the use of the renminbi (RMB) as an invoicing currency, and how Chinese foreign aid affects China's exports.
